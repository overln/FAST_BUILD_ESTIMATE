Scope of Work

Where bracing elements are required in wet areas we have allowed to swap out 10mm Braceline for 10mm Aqualine

We have assumed full height tiling or joinery in areas with Aqualine, we have allowed to undercoat these walls only.

No allowance has been made to apply stain finish to base build.

We've allowed to stop all walls and ceilings to a level 4 finish, per the manufactures specification.

We have allowed to paint all interior walls, ceilings, doors, and trims in Resene paint finish or equally approved spec.

No allowance made for exterior painting/staining and stopping - removed from scope (advise from the client).

No allowance has been made to supply GIB linings, fixings, and glue - we assume the main contractor or client to supply.

No allowance has been made for access; we assume the builder/main contractor will provide height access.

We have allowed to install all plasterboard linings per the manufacturer's specification and GIB bracing guide.

Terms of Service

Any alteration of agreed working hours or design may cause a delay to the agreed completion date.

Areas receiving finishes shall have unimpeded access.

Our estimate is valid for 30 days after submission.

A monthly late payment fee of 7% will be charged for payments not received within 14 days of the due date.


Standard exclusions:


We have made no allowance for finishes to joinery, railings, surfaces, or areas not specified in our proposal or markup.

We have made no allowance to split the trade package offered.

We've made no allowance for works above 3m; we assume height access by the main contractor.

No allowance has been made for parking, to be charged at zero margin.

Unless noted, there is no allowance for any speciality finishes such as intumescent coating, weather coatings and the like.

Any touch-ups or patches due to others will be deemed a Variation unless due to our workmanship.

The above costs are GST-exclusive.
